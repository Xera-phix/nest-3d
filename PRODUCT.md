# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Design-conscious renters and homeowners use Afterglow on a laptop or tablet while comparing real furniture layouts. They need to move from visual exploration to trustworthy measurements without learning professional CAD software.

## Product Purpose

Afterglow makes one small room feel tangible before anything heavy moves. Success means a user can understand the room immediately, try several arrangements quickly, and trust that selected furniture remains within measured room bounds.

## Brand Personality

Tactile, precise, composed. The experience should feel calm and materially rich, with the quiet confidence of an architecture studio and none of a game editor's visual noise.

## Anti-references

- Neon gaming HUDs, dark sci-fi editors, and floating glass control panels.
- Generic SaaS dashboards built from interchangeable cards and explanatory copy.
- Flat CAD software that treats the room as geometry without atmosphere.
- Photorealistic renderers that sacrifice interaction speed for decorative effects.

## Design Principles

1. The room is the interface. Controls support the model instead of framing or competing with it.
2. Earn trust through visible scale. Measurements, snapping, bounds, and collisions are clear at the moment they matter.
3. Make spatial changes feel continuous. Camera and object motion preserve orientation rather than teleporting the user.
4. Put material character in the room and restraint in the controls.
5. Keep the common workflow immediate: select, move, compare, and return.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA for DOM controls and text. Support keyboard-only selection and transforms, screen-reader status announcements, 44 px touch targets, reduced motion, reduced transparency, and redundant color-plus-shape feedback for collisions and selection.
